Two documents disagree. Which one still counts?

Ask a question about the sample college handbook below. Palimpsest retrieves the passages, decides which ones were written over, and shows you what the answering model is allowed to see. When it cannot tell, it says so.

All documents are fictional. Answers are information, not legal or academic advice.

Privacy

This demo runs on your own computer. The decision model (Strands Decider 2B) is served from your machine and the page talks only to a server on 127.0.0.1.

Your question is held in memory while it is checked and then discarded. The server writes no logs of questions and keeps no database, cookies or analytics. The page loads no fonts, scripts or images from other sites.

If you connect the optional Amazon Bedrock agent, only the passages that survived the gate and your question are sent to Bedrock, under your own AWS account and its terms. Passages marked as out of date are never sent.

Do not put personal data in the sample corpus. If you point Palimpsest at your own documents, they stay in the folder you choose.
