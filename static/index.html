Two documents disagree. Which one still counts?

Ask a question about the sample college handbook. Palimpsest finds the passages and decides which ones were written over. Then it shows what the answering model is allowed to see. When it cannot tell it says so.

All documents are fictional. Answers are for information only.

Privacy

This demo runs on your own computer. The decision model is served from your machine and the page only talks to a server on 127.0.0.1.

Your question stays in memory while it is checked and is then discarded. The server keeps no question logs. It has no database and no cookies and no analytics. The page loads nothing from other sites.

The optional Amazon Bedrock agent sends only your question and the passages that passed the gate. That happens under your own AWS account and its terms. Passages marked out of date are never sent.

Do not put personal data in the sample corpus. Your own documents stay in the folder you choose.
